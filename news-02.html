Was part of नवसृजन — the Design Innovation Workshop at IIT Jodhpur, jointly organised by Jodhpur City Knowledge and Innovation Foundation (JCKIF), National Institute of Fashion Technology (NIFT) Jodhpur, and Footwear Design and Development Institute (FDDI) Jodhpur.

The workshop brought together 100 artisans — 50 from the textile sector and 50 from the leather craft community — who brought their stories, skills, and passion for craft to the sessions.

In the session on Market Trends and Consumer Behaviour, we explored how changing consumer preferences, storytelling, and thoughtful presentation can help crafts reach newer markets and broader audiences.
