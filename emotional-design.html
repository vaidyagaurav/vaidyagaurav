Research Area

Emotional Design

This strand of work investigates how products elicit affective responses — pleasure, surprise, trust, frustration — and how these responses can be anticipated and designed for rather than left to chance.

The aim is to build frameworks that help designers move beyond usability and aesthetics alone, toward products that support meaningful emotional experiences across use, ownership, and disposal.

Focus Areas

Affective response frameworks

Adapting and extending established models of product emotion to identify how form, material, and interaction cues shape user response.

Emotion-led design methods

Developing tools and workshop formats that help design students and practitioners translate emotional intent into concrete design decisions.

Evaluation of emotional experience

Using qualitative and mixed-method approaches to assess how users emotionally engage with product concepts and finished designs.
