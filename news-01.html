About the Event

I had the opportunity to conduct a session on Toy-based Integrated Pedagogy for Mathematics as part of the STEM Teacher Training Program for PM SHRI Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, organised by IIT Jodhpur and the Navodaya Leadership Institute, Udaipur.

The session brought together 50 teachers from Delhi, Rajasthan, and Haryana, exploring how play-based and toy-driven approaches can make mathematical concepts more intuitive and engaging for school students.
