Assistant Professor Dr. Gaurav Vinodrao Vaidya

Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur PhD, Dept. of Design, IIT Guwahati
M.Des., IDC School of Design, IIT Bombay
B.E. (Mechanical Engineering), YCCE Nagpur

I am a Design Educator and Researcher working at the intersection of emotional design, product form, and material culture. My primary research interest lies in the question of why and how consumer products evoke emotions. I develop tools and methods that facilitate an approach for emotion-driven design, and examine how design education can cultivate practitioners who integrate intellectual rigour with hands-on making.

Research

Research Areas

Emotional Design

Investigating affective responses to product artefacts and developing frameworks for emotion-led design of products, including smart wearables.

Product Form & Semantics

Exploring how visual form communicates meaning — using product semantics to guide the design of emerging product categories such as smartwatches.

Ideation Methods

Developing and evaluating creative ideation tools — including Figures of Speech card tools — to support innovative product form generation.

Design & Craft

Examining the epistemological and cultural dimensions of craft knowledge and its integration into contemporary product design practice.

Design Education

Developing pedagogical frameworks for design research, including tools for research opportunity identification in engineering and product design education.
