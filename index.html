Dr. Gaurav Vinodrao Vaidya

Assistant Professor
School of Design
Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur

I am a Design Educator and Researcher working at the intersection of emotional design, material culture and design education. My primary research interest lies in the question of why and how consumer products evoke emotions. I develop tools and methods that facilitate an approach for emotion-driven design, and examine how design education can cultivate practitioners who integrate intellectual rigour with hands-on making.
